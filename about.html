Research Engineer — AI Evaluations & Agentic Safety

I design empirical evaluations for agentic AI systems and study behavioural measurement, adversarial failure modes, memory integrity, and evaluation robustness.

The aim is to make claims about agent behaviour testable: define the construct, expose the system to controlled and adversarial conditions, measure outcomes across repeated runs, and report where the result stops generalising.

Research trajectory

Systems engineering → agent memory → adversarial benchmarking → evaluation methodology → agentic AI safety.

More than a decade in data engineering and business intelligence established a systems view of reliability, observability, data lineage, and failure recovery. Building stateful agent systems then made memory a measurement problem: what was stored, what was retrieved, what crossed a boundary, and how those choices altered later behaviour.

That work now informs adversarial benchmarks and evaluation methodology. I am interested in experiments that distinguish system capability from benchmark artefacts, surface behavioural changes under attack or distribution shift, and remain robust to evaluator, prompt, model, and context-policy choices.

Memory Quality Under Attack

This research thread examines a clean LongMemEval measurement alongside false-fact poisoning, utility under the tested attack, trust-weighted retrieval, and the tested default defense. I am withholding findings and numerical claims until the direct public benchmark artifact can be verified.

Current questions

  • Which behavioural measures are valid, reproducible, and useful for stateful agents?
  • How do memory contamination, attribution loss, and access-boundary failures propagate into action?
  • Which adversarial cases reveal systematic failure modes rather than isolated anecdotes?
  • How sensitive are evaluation conclusions to prompts, judges, baselines, and task construction?

Selected provenance

Aegis Memory is the principal engineering artefact behind the memory-integrity work: a persistent memory system for multi-agent AI with scoped access boundaries. Quantify Labs Ltd. is the UK-registered organisation through which that work has been developed. These provide implementation provenance for the research direction rather than define it.
